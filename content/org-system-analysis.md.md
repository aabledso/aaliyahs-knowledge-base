# Analyzing an Organizing System

## **Public Knowledge Base:** Wikipedia
### Primary Resources
---
Wikipedia's primary resources are various articles about different topics. These articles have pictures and references that support the information being provided by the articles. Users are able to find articles about almost any topic they can imagine. 
### Primary Interactions
---
* #### Important searches
* #### Finding sources of information
* #### Browsing new topics

Many users use Wikipedia to narrow down their search on different topics. Wikipedia provides articles that cover topics which can then be used to find more specific information that the user is looking for. The articles on Wikipedia have many hyperlinks that allow users to deep dive into their topic and also provide new keywords that can be used to discover more information. Many people use Wikipedia as a sort of introduction to their chosen topic. 

### Classification System
---
Wikipedia uses a primarily **hierarchical** system of classification, but also **faceted** as well through its use of filters and search tools. 

Wikipedia's main menu is organized into categories such as main page, contents, current events, and random article. After clicking on one of the main menu categories, users are able to narrow down their search through browsing options such as subject, format, and quality. There is also a search bar option for users and many filters that help them narrow down their searches.

### Relationships
---
**Hyperlinks** are the main way in which Wikipedia establishes relationships between the resources being represented. There are hyperlinks all throughout the website related to the content you're currently viewing or to other content groups. It is also important to note that related resources are in the same main categories, making it easier for users to only view content that's related to what they are looking for.
