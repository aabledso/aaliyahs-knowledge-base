## What is Espresso?

Espresso is the foundation of many of my favorite coffee drinks, especially [[3. La La Land|La La Land's]]. On it's own, espresso has a very strong flavor, but it is also used as a base in many drinks.

I personally had to learn to love espresso. At first I found it too strong for my liking and it left a bad taste in my mouth. After trying it in different [[Specialty Coffee|specialty coffees]], I was able to ease y way into liking it.

## Best pairings:

Since espresso has a strong and bold flavor, I think it pairs best with slightly sweet foods such as a muffin from [[2. On the Grind| On the Grind]]. I find that the flavors balance each other well and will sometimes even pair it with a [[Savory Bites|Savory Bite]].
