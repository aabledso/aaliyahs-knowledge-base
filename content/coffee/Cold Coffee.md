## What is Cold Coffee?

Cold coffee is usually my first choice when it's warm outside or when I want soemthing refreshing instead of [[Espresso]] or [[Drip Coffee]]. Most coffee shops have many cold coffee options ranging from cold brews to iced lattes. I like that they usually can be customized so I can request it exactly as I'd like.

## My Favorite Cold Coffee Drinks

If I'm visiting a coffee shop for the first time, I almost always order an iced vanilla latte. I tink it's a great way to see how good their [[Espresso]] is without being overwhelmed by too many flavors

* Iced Vanilla Latte
* Campfire Cold Brew
* Iced White Chocolate Mocha
* Cold Brew w/ splash of heavy whipping cream
