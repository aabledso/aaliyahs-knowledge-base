## What are Specialty Coffees?

I believe specialty coffees are any coffees that are seasonal or specific to that coffee shop. Instead of sticking t a simple cup of coffee, these drinks combine espresso with different type of milk, flavored syrups, and whipped topping to make something more fun.

## My Favorite Specialty Coffees

* Caramel Macchiatos
* White Chocolate Mocha
* Brown Sugar Oatmilk Shaken Espresso*
* Caramel Frappe
