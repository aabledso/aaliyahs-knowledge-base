## What is Drip Coffee?

Drip coffee is one of the simplest and most classic drinks you'll find at any coffee shop. It's made by pouring hot water over ground coffee beans and then allowing the brewed coffee to to drip from a filter to a pot. Even though it's relatively basic, I think it's one of the best ways to experience the natural taste of different varieties of coffee.

## When I Order It

I usually order drip coffee when I need a reliable caffeine boost. While it's often not as tasty as a fancy [[Specialty Coffee]] or a [[RedBull Energy Drinks| RedBull Energy Drink]], it is reliable and I have confidence that each time I order it'll taste the same.

## Best Pairings

Drip coffee goes well with just about anything. Usually after finishing a nice cup of drip coffee, I start to get a little hungry. That being said, I recommend any of the small-bites your coffee shop offers.
