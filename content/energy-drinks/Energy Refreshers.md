## What are Energy Refreshers?

Energy refreshers are my go-to when I need something lighter than a [[Specialty Coffee]], but that gives me energy the same way [[Espresso|espresso]] does. They're usually made with a caffeinated energy base that's mixed with fruit flavor, lemonade, or sparkling water. Unlike tradition energy drinks, Energy Refreshers often taste like a fruity juice or soda.

## My Favorite Flavors:

These are a few combinations I'd recommend if it's your first time trying Energy Refreshers.
* Charged Strawberry Lemonade
* Watermelon **+** Kiwi refresher
* Passionfruit **+** Pineapple
