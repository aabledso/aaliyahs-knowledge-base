## What is a Frozen Energy Drink?

Frozen energy drinks are one of my favorite alternatives to [[Cold Coffee|cold coffee]], especially during the hot summer months. They're made by blending ice with an energy drink such as RedBull along with flavored syrups to create a thick drink similar to an Icee. They're just as versatile as [[RedBull Energy Drinks]] and you can usually get them with any flavors you can imagine.

## My Favorite Flavor Combos

* Peach **+** Mango
* Blue Raspberry **+** Lime
* Passion Fruit **+** Coconut
* Watermelon **+** Lime
