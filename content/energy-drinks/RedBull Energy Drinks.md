## Breaking Down RedBull Energy Drinks

Energy drinks made with RedBull are top-tier. They are versatile and mesh well with any flavored syrups you choose to add. You can even get them in [[Frozen Energy Drinks|frozen energy drinks]]. Sometimes shops will have recommended favor combos or sometimes the If you aren't a fan of RedBull, [[1. Dutch Bros|Dutch Bros]] has their own drink similar to RedBull, but a little less acidic.

## My Favorite Flavor Combos:

I enjoy at least two fruity flavors and am happy with either frozen or iced.
* Strawberry **+** Orange
* Blue Raspberry **+** Strawberry **+** Almond
* Lime **+** Vanilla
