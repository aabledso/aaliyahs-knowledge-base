## What are Pastries?

Pastries include items such as croissants, scones, or muffins. These are only a few pastries, but some coffee shops, such as [[4. Summer Moon| Summer Moon]] offer a lot more options. Really, a pastry is a baked good made from a dough of some sort. They are usually sweet, but can be slightly savory as well. Some shops make their pastries in-store while others receive them from a central kitchen.
## Best Pairings

Pastries pair well with a classic Americano [[Drip Coffee|drip coffee]]. A sweet pastry in between sips of a bitter, piping hot Americano is usually my go-to. I've also seen people pair pastries with teas such as [[Green Tea|green tea]] or [[Black Tea|black tea]].
