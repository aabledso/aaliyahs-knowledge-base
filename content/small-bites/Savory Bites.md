## What are Savory Bites?

I consider savory bites to be small handheld foods that provide an alternative when you're not in the mood for [[Pastries|pastries]]. Unlike muffins or sweet filled croissants, savory bites usually contain ingredients such as eggs, meats, and/or vegetables. I usually get them when I'm feeling hungry but already ordering a sweet [[Specialty Coffee|coffee]].
