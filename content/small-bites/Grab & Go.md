## What's a Grab & Go?

Grab and Go's are the small pre-packaged foods that are seen lining the checkout counters or on a display somewhere within most coffee shops. They usually have small snacks such as cookies, snack trays, yogurt parfaits, or even boiled eggs. You usually pick them up and the workers will ring you up at the register. These are convenient for busy workdays, short lunch breaks, or simply when you need something a little more filling than a [[Savory Bites|savory bite]]. It is cool because every coffee shop usually ahs their own unique selection of Grab and Go items.

## Best Pairings

I really enjoy pairing Grab and Go items with drinks that won't overpower them. A [[Drip Coffee|drip coffee]] is my usual choice but sometimes I'll pair them with an iced [[Specialty Coffee]] if it's especially hot that day.
## My Favorites:

* Stack of cookies **+** [[Drip Coffee]]
* Yogurt parfait **+** [[Black Tea]]
* Snack Tray **+** [[Green Tea]]
