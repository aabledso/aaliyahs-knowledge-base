## Why On The Grind Is #2

On the Grind comes in 2nd place because their options are limited compared to [[Dutch Bros]]. They have amazing [[Specialty Coffee]], but also have reliable [[Iced Teas]] as well. I like that On the Grind is close to my house. It makes it easy to stop by on the way to work or on the way home from work if I'm working day shifts. Their customer service is also just as friendly as Dutch.

## My Go-To Order

When stopping by On the Grind, I usually opt for an iced peach [[Green Tea]] or an iced [[Black Tea]] with raspberry flavored syrup.

## Must Try:

* New iced matcha
* Hot white chocolate mocha with whipped cream
* Grasshopper hot or iced
