## Summer Moon

Summer Moon Coffee actually has a really calm ambiance. Although it does get pretty busy, everything still feels calm. I usually go to Summer Moon when I have time to sit down and get the full coffee shop experience. Sometimes I'll sit, have a [[Savory Bites|savory bite]], sip on an Americano, and enjoy being present.

## My Go-To

At Summer Moon, I usually get a hot Americano with a cream cheese bagel on the side.

##  Must Try:

* Frozen Hot Chocolate
* Iced Matcha Moon Latte
* Velvet Blaze [[Drip Coffee|Drip Coffee]]
