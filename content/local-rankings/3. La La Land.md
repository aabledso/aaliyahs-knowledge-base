## La La Land as #3

I visit La La Land less frequently than I do the other places in [[local-rankings/index|Local Rankings]] because it is rather expensive. That being said their Cloud Lattes are absolutely amazing! They are visually appealing and taste even better than they look.

## My Order

I enjoy ordering their Lavender Bloom Matcha Latte. A smooth matcha paired with the vibrant and fresh flavor of lavender has never failed me!

## Must Try:

* Upside Down Latte with single shot [[Espresso|espresso]] and almond milk
* Strawberry Cloud Chai
* MAKA Toast
