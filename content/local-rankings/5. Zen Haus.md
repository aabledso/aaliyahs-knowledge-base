## Zen Haus

Zen Haus, may be number 5 on my Local Rankings list, but it is still an amazing coffee shop. IN this shop they have fun couches, lamps, and signs that make the whole experience 10x better. Their main focus is Match, but they have [[Cold Coffee]], [[Drip Coffee]], and [[Teas/index|Teas]] as well.

## My Go-To Order

I go to Zen Haus when I'm craving a Matcha. I mainly get their Ube Matcha but have been looking to try their Busy Bee Smash.

## Must Try:

* Matchana Split
* Earl Grey Chiffon Cake [[Pastries|pastry]]
* Jamaica Lemonade
