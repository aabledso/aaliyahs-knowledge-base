# Iced Teas

## What do Iced Teas taste like?
Much like Herbal Teas, the flavor of Iced Teas largely depends on what you sue to make them. Upon taking a sip of an Iced Tea, you are hit with a refreshing taste that makes the Summer heat a little less unbearable. Your Iced Tea can be sweet, unsweet, strong, creamy, or fruity. [[Herbal Teas]], [[Green Tea|Green Teas]], and [[Chai Tea|Chai Teas]] all make great Iced Teas as well. Iced Teas are really a build-your-own type of tea and can carry any flavor profile you desire.
