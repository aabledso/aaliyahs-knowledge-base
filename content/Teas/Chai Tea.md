# Chai Tea

## What does Chai Tea taste like?
Chai Tea tastes similar to the feeling of Fall. It is warm, creamy, and has a little bit of a kick to it. Chai Tea is from the [[Black Tea|Black Tea]] family, but it has added spices such as cinnamon and cloves that I believe make it taste completely different from an ordinary Black Tea. I prefer to pair Chai Tea with milk and sugar, but sometimes opt for a familiar and basic Chai Latte on days I'm feeling indecisive. 

### My favorites:
- Dirty Chai with double the espresso
- Chai Latte with almond flavor added
- Plain Chai Latte from CommonWealth Coffee House


>Chai Tea to fill your heart with warmth!
