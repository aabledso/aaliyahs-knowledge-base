# Green Tea

## What does Green Tea taste like?
Green Tea tastes very light and is very customizable and versatile. It is best paired with fruity flavors such as peach or cherry, but can also be paired with heavy cream. In my opinion, it tastes a little bit like grass, but that could just be the light green color. Whether it's in an [[Iced Teas|Iced Tea]] or piping hot, it still tastes just as good. I prefer drinking Green Tea when I'm in need of a small pick me up to get me motivated to complete tasks or run errands.
### My favorites:
- Green Tea with coconut milk and coconut flavor from Dutch Bros
- Matcha with vanilla and lavender
- Cherry Blossom Green Tea from 7Brew

>Green Tea is for Spring cleaning days!
