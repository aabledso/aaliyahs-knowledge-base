# Herbal Teas

## What do Herbal Teas taste like?
Herbal Teas do not have one set flavor. Instead, they taste like whatever herbs or flowers were used to make them. Depending on the herbal tea you choose, the flavors can be sweet and fruity like summer, minty, or even spicy. Often times herbal teas do pretty well on their own. There's no need to add artificial flavors, sugar, or cream, but that's definitely an option if that's what you prefer. I prefer to drink herbal teas that have at least two flavors in the name because I appreciate when the flavors are well balanced. I usually opt for herbal teas when I'm feeling sick because I find them satiating.
