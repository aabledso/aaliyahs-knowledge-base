# Black Tea

## What does Black Tea taste like?
Black tea tastes very strong similar to that of watered down cold brew. It is a lot more bold than [[Green Tea|Green Tea]] and can sometimes taste fairly bitter if you steep it for too long. With black tea, it pairs well with nuttier or creamy flavors, but it even tastes great plain. If you are new to trying Black Tea, I'd recommend ordering a plain one first and then adding the same flavors you'd add to your usual cup of coffee. This will give you a familiar taste that you can then customize and build on after trying your choice of Black Tea.

### My favorites:
- Earl Grey with splash of heavy cream
- Lord Bergamot from Honey Flutters
- Yemeni Black Tea from Qatra
